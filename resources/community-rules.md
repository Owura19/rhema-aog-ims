# DRAFT — for the Pastor to approve

**Community Guidelines** · version 2026-11-draft

Welcome to the Rhema Assembly of God community. This is a place to encourage one another, share what God is doing and stay connected through the week. To keep it safe and kind for everyone, we ask you to follow these guidelines.

## Be kind
- Speak to others the way you would in church: with love, patience and respect (Ephesians 4:29).
- Disagree gently. Talk about ideas, not people.

## Not allowed here
- Hate, insults or mocking anyone for their tribe, background, gender, age or disability.
- Harassment, bullying or threats.
- Sexual content of any kind.
- Scams, money requests, selling or advertising.
- Political campaigning.

## Protect people's privacy
- Don't share other people's private information: phone numbers, addresses, health or family matters.
- Don't post photos of children without a parent's consent.
- Only post photos of other adults if they are happy for you to share them.

## If something is wrong
- Use **Report** on any post or comment that breaks these guidelines. Reports go to the church's moderators.
- You can **block** someone so you no longer see each other's posts.

## Moderators
Church moderators may hide or remove posts and comments, and may stop someone from posting, to keep the community safe.

Thank you for helping us make this a place where everyone feels welcome.
